Babies are vulnerable to sleep-related hazards, including the risk of suffocation caused by soft bedding covering or obstructing their breathing area. This project focuses on developing a Smart Baby Monitoring System using Internet of Things (IoT) technology to help caregivers monitor a baby’s sleeping environment and identify potential soft bedding suffocation risks. A review of previous studies and existing baby monitoring systems was carried out to understand the main risk factors and identify suitable sensors and technologies for the proposed system. Based on these findings, an IoT-based monitoring system was designed and developed to monitor selected conditions around the baby during sleep. When the system detects a condition that may indicate a potential suffocation risk, it sends an alert to the caregiver. The developed system is evaluated through a series of tests to assess its functionality, ability to detect potential risks, and alert response time. The project aims to provide a simple and practical monitoring solution that can assist caregivers in creating a safer sleeping environment for babies.
